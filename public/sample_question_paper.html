SOLIDWORKS SKILL ASSESSMENT - MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS

Time: 45 Minutes Total Questions: 10 MCQs Max Marks: 20 Marks
1. Which feature is used to create a 3D solid from a 2D sketch by adding thickness?
A) Extruded Boss/Base
B) Revolved Boss/Base
C) Swept Boss/Base
D) Lofted Boss/Base
Answer: A
2. What is the shortcut key to rebuild the active model in SolidWorks?
A) Ctrl + S
B) Ctrl + B
C) Ctrl + Q
D) Ctrl + R
Answer: B
3. Which plane is NOT one of the three default reference planes in SolidWorks?
A) Front Plane
B) Top Plane
C) Right Plane
D) Bottom Plane
Answer: D
4. What does the Fillet feature do to edges or internal faces?
A) Creates sharp corners
B) Rounds internal or external edges
C) Creates a drilled hole
D) Scales the 3D part
Answer: B
5. Which geometric relation forces two circles to share the exact same center point?
A) Concentric
B) Coincident
C) Collinear
D) Coradial
Answer: A
6. What file extension is used for a standard SolidWorks Part file?
A) .sldasm
B) .slddrw
C) .sldprt
D) .dwg
Answer: C
7. What color indicates that a sketch in SolidWorks is Fully Defined?
A) Blue
B) Red
C) Black
D) Yellow
Answer: C
8. Which tool is used to remove material along a guided 2D/3D sketch path?
A) Swept Cut
B) Extruded Cut
C) Revolved Cut
D) Lofted Cut
Answer: A
9. In SolidWorks Assembly mode, which relationship controls relative motion between parts?
A) Relations
B) Mates
C) Constraints
D) Dimensions
Answer: B
10. What is the standard angle for an Isometric projection view?
A) 30 degrees
B) 45 degrees
C) 60 degrees
D) 90 degrees
Answer: A
