20 Cricket Objective Questions

1. How many players are there in a cricket team?
A) 9 B) 10
C) 11 D) 12
Answer: C

2. How many legal balls are there in a standard over?
A) 4 B) 5
C) 6 D) 8
Answer: C

3. How many stumps are there in one wicket?
A) 2 B) 3
C) 4 D) 6
Answer: B

4. How many runs are scored when the ball crosses the boundary without bouncing?
A) 2 B) 3
C) 4 D) 6
Answer: D

5. How many runs are scored when the ball reaches the boundary after bouncing?
A) 1 B) 2
C) 4 D) 6
Answer: C

6. What does LBW stand for?
A) Leg Before Wicket B) Long Ball Wide
C) Left Bat Wicket D) Leg Behind Wicket
Answer: A

7. Who stands behind the stumps to catch the ball?
A) Bowler B) Wicketkeeper
C) Umpire D) Coach
Answer: B

8. What is a century in cricket?
A) 25 runs B) 50 runs
C) 100 runs D) 200 runs
Answer: C

9. What does ODI stand for?
A) One Day International B) One Delivery Innings
C) Official Day Inning D) Open Day International
Answer: A

10. How many overs does each team usually have in a T20 innings?
A) 10 B) 20
C) 40 D) 50
Answer: B

11. How many overs does each team usually have in an ODI innings?
A) 20 B) 25
C) 40 D) 50
Answer: D

12. What is a hat-trick in cricket?
A) Three fours in a row B) Three sixes in a row
C) Three wickets with consecutive deliveries
D) Three maiden overs
Answer: C

13. What is a maiden over?
A) An over with no runs charged to the bowler
B) An over with six sixes
C) An over with a wicket every ball
D) The first over of a match
Answer: A

14. What does the toss decide?
A) Who wins the match
B) Which captain chooses to bat or bowl first
C) Who scores a century
D) How many players are on the field
Answer: B

15. Which player usually delivers the ball to the batter?
A) Bowler B) Wicketkeeper
C) Umpire D) Coach
Answer: A

16. What is a run out?
A) A batter is dismissed while out of their ground
B) A ball goes for six
C) A bowler bowls a wide
D) A batter scores 100 runs
Answer: A

17. What is a no-ball?
A) A legal delivery B) An illegal delivery under the rules
C) A ball hit for six D) A completed over
Answer: B

18. What is a wide ball?
A) A delivery too far from the batter to be played fairly
B) A ball that hits the stumps
C) A ball hit to the boundary
D) A ball caught by the wicketkeeper
Answer: A

19. How many innings does each team usually have in a limited-overs match?
A) One B) Two
C) Three D) Four
Answer: A

20. Who makes decisions on the field and signals decisions such as out or wide?
A) Spectator B) Umpire
C) Groundskeeper D) Commentator
Answer: B
